# Lyle Light

**A quiet space for your words.** 简洁、清晰，专注于阅读与写作。

Inter pairs with Source Han Sans SC for comfortable English and Chinese text. Blue links, subtle rules and generous spacing keep the page easy to scan.

## Two ways to write

| Theme | Content width | Best for |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Lyle Light | 1000px | Everyday reading and writing |
| Lyle Light Wide | 1600px | Wide tables, code and large screens |
